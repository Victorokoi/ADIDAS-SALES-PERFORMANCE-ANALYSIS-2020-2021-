# ADIDAS-SALES-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS-2020-2021-
## A Comparative Business Intelligence and Sales Performance Analysis
## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses Adidas' sales performance across 2020 and 2021, examining how the company's business performance changed over the two-year period.
The analysis focuses on four major performance indicators: total sales, operating profit, units sold, and operating margin. It also investigates retailer contributions, geographical sales distribution, and monthly sales patterns.
Using Microsoft Power BI, I developed an interactive dashboard to compare performance across both years, identify business trends, and uncover insights that could support strategic decision-making.
The central objective was to understand how Adidas performed, which retail partners contributed most to revenue, and how sales patterns changed between 2020 and 2021.

## 2. Overall Business Performance
The analysis revealed a significant increase in Adidas' sales performance between 2020 and 2021.
Key Performance Indicator	2020	2021
Total Sales	$182M	$718M
Operating Profit	$63M	$269M
Units Sold	462K	2M
Operating Margin	52,566%*	356K%*
The operating margin figures are displayed as shown in the dashboard but require verification of the underlying DAX calculations and percentage formatting.
Key Findings
•	Revenue growth: Total sales increased from $182 million in 2020 to $718 million in 2021, representing an approximate 295% increase.
•	Profit growth: Operating profit increased from $63 million to $269 million, representing approximately 327% growth.
•	Sales volume growth: Units sold increased from 462,000 to approximately 2 million, indicating a substantial increase in sales volume.
These results suggest that Adidas experienced considerable growth in revenue, profitability, and sales volume in 2021 compared with 2020.

## 5. Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
The monthly sales comparison reveals different patterns of performance across the two years.
2020 Monthly Sales Performance
The 2020 monthly trend showed considerable fluctuations.
Key observations:
•	January recorded approximately $16 million in sales.
•	Sales declined slightly in February before recovering in March.
•	April recorded the highest monthly sales, approaching $25 million.
•	Sales dropped significantly in May and June, with June recording approximately $9 million.
•	July and August experienced a recovery, with August approaching $20 million.
•	Sales declined again towards the end of the year, reaching approximately $8 million in December.
The most noticeable feature of 2020 was the sharp decline following the April sales peak.
2021 Monthly Sales Performance
The 2021 monthly trend showed a different pattern.
Key observations:
•	January started at approximately $55 million.
•	Sales declined through February and reached their lowest point in March, at approximately $39 million.
•	Sales recovered significantly between April and July.
•	July recorded one of the strongest sales performances, approaching $78 million.
•	Sales declined from August through October.
•	November and December experienced a recovery, with December again approaching $78 million.
Comparison of Monthly Trends
Comparing both years reveals several important observations:
1.	Higher monthly sales in 2021: The monthly sales figures in 2021 were substantially higher than those in 2020.
2.	Different peak months: April was the strongest month in 2020, while July and December were among the strongest months in 2021.
3.	Sales fluctuations: Both years experienced periods of declining and recovering sales, indicating that performance was not consistent throughout the year.
4.	December recovery: Both years displayed a recovery or relatively strong performance around December, although the strength of this pattern differed considerably.
Business implication: Adidas should investigate the factors behind its strongest sales months, including seasonal demand, marketing campaigns, promotions, and product availability. These findings could help improve sales forecasting and inventory planning.

## 6. Product Performance Analysis
Product performance was evaluated using operating profit and units sold to identify the categories contributing most significantly to business results.
2020 Findings
Men's Street Footwear emerged as the leading product category, generating approximately $15.6 million in operating profit and recording 99,000 units sold.
Women's Apparel followed with approximately $11.3 million in operating profit, while Men's Apparel generated $9.7 million.
2021 Findings
Men's Street Footwear maintained its leading position, generating approximately $67 million in operating profit and recording approximately 490,000 units sold.
Women's Apparel followed with approximately $57 million in operating profit, while Men's Athletic Footwear generated approximately $42 million.
Key Product Insights
1.	Men's Street Footwear maintained its position as the highest-performing category across both years.
2.	Women's Apparel demonstrated strong performance, becoming the second-highest contributor to operating profit in 2021.
3.	Men's Athletic Footwear also recorded significant operating profit in 2021.
4.	The leading product categories contributed significantly to overall business performance.
Business Implication
Adidas should continue evaluating inventory allocation, product demand, and marketing investment across its strongest product categories while identifying opportunities to improve the performance of less-contributing categories.
## 7. Sales Channel Analysis
The analysis examined three major sales channels:
•	In-store
•	Outlet
•	Online
The objective was to understand how each channel contributed to the company's total sales distribution.
Sales Channel Distribution
Sales Channel	2020	2021
In-store	51.57%	36.60%
Outlet	45.95%	29.52%
Online	2.48%	33.87%
Key Findings
In-store Sales
In-store sales accounted for 51.57% of sales distribution in 2020, making it the leading channel. Its contribution declined to 36.60% in 2021.
Outlet Sales
Outlet sales contributed 45.95% in 2020 but declined to 29.52% in 2021.
Online Sales
The most significant change occurred in online sales. Its contribution increased from just 2.48% in 2020 to 33.87% in 2021.
The findings reveal a considerable shift in sales channel distribution.
Although in-store sales remained the largest individual channel in 2021, online sales became a major contributor to the company's overall sales distribution.
This presents an opportunity for Adidas to strengthen its digital sales strategy while maintaining effective operations across physical retail channels.

## Business Insights
1. Adidas recorded substantial growth in sales revenue, operating profit, and units sold in 2021 compared with 2020.
2. West Gear was the leading retailer in 2020, while Foot Locker took the lead in 2021. This demonstrates that retailer contributions can change considerably over time.
3. Foot Locker and Sports Direct recorded particularly strong sales performances in 2021, making them important channels for further business evaluation.
4. The strongest sales months differed between the two years. This highlights the importance of monitoring monthly performance rather than relying exclusively on annual totals.
5. The geographical analysis provides a foundation for examining regional sales distribution and identifying opportunities for targeted market strategies.
6. Men's Street Footwear led product performance, generating $15.6 million in operating profit and recording 99,000 units sold.
7. Women's Apparel followed with $11.3 million in operating profit.
8. In-store sales accounted for 51.57% of total sales, while outlet sales contributed 45.95%.
9. Online sales accounted for 2.48% of the sales distribution, highlighting an opportunity to investigate digital sales growth.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. Maintain adequate inventory and targeted marketing strategies for high-performing categories, particularly Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel.

2. Build on the increase in online sales contribution by improving e-commerce operations, digital marketing, customer engagement, and the online purchasing experience.

3. Evaluate the strategies responsible for the strong performance of Foot Locker and Sports Direct in 2021 and identify opportunities to strengthen other retail partnerships.

4. Evaluate the performance of in-store, outlet, and online channels to identify opportunities for improved operational efficiency and revenue generation.

5. Use historical monthly sales patterns to improve demand forecasting, inventory management, and promotional planning.

6. Use state-level sales information to guide regional marketing investments, identify growth opportunities, and improve distribution planning.

7. Investigate the factors responsible for the improvement in operating profit and operating margin to identify strategies that can sustain profitability as revenue grows.

8. Continue monitoring product profitability and sales volume to support better inventory allocation, marketing investment, and business decisions.
