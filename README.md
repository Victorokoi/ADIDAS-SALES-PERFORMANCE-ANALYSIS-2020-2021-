# ADIDAS-SALES-PERFORMANCE-ANALYSIS-2020-2021-
A Comparative Business Intelligence and Sales Performance Analysis
## 1. Project Overview
This project analyses Adidas' sales performance across 2020 and 2021, examining how the company's business performance changed over the two-year period.
The analysis focuses on four major performance indicators: total sales, operating profit, units sold, and operating margin. It also investigates retailer contributions, geographical sales distribution, and monthly sales patterns.
Using Microsoft Power BI, I developed an interactive dashboard to compare performance across both years, identify business trends, and uncover insights that could support strategic decision-making.
The central objective was to understand how Adidas performed, which retail partners contributed most to revenue, and how sales patterns changed between 2020 and 2021.

## 2. Overall Business Performance
The analysis revealed a significant increase in Adidas' sales performance between 2020 and 2021.
